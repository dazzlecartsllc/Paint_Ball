Privacy Policy — Paint Roll

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Developer: Mini Island Studio
Listed on Google Play as "Paint Roll: Color Maze Puzzle" · Package: com.minigameisland.paintroll

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Paint Roll mobile game ("Paint Roll", "the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using Paint Roll, you agree to the practices described here.

What Paint Roll does today

Current version

Paint Roll is a single-player maze puzzle game. It currently does not require an account or sign-up to play.

In its current version, Paint Roll does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All gameplay information — level progress, unlocked ball skins, unlocked themes/textures, and your in-game coin balance — is stored locally on your device only (using Android's standard app-preferences storage) and is never sent to us or to any third party. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Data collection once ads & analytics are added

Upcoming

We plan to integrate Google AdMob (for in-app advertising) and Firebase Analytics (for basic app-usage insights) in an upcoming update. This policy already describes that future data collection so it stays accurate once those features ship — we will update the "Effective date" above if the scope changes further.

Once integrated, the following third-party services may collect data on our behalf:

  • Google AdMob — used to show in-app advertising. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, approximate location, device information, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from AdMob.
  • Firebase Analytics — used to understand how players use the app (e.g. which levels are played, session length, crashes). It may collect device information, app-usage events, and approximate location. See Firebase's Privacy and Security policy.

We do not sell your data. We do not have our own servers or backend — any data collected by the services above is collected and processed directly by Google under its own privacy terms linked above.

In-app purchases

Paint Roll currently has no real-money in-app purchases — all skins and themes are unlocked with coins earned by playing. If real-money purchases are introduced in the future, they will be processed entirely by Google Play Billing, and this policy will be updated accordingly; we will never see or store your payment details.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization: you can opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising ID, from your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google Settings → Ads on Android), and from Google's own Ad Settings.
  • Local data: since progress is stored only on your device, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Paint Roll removes all of it. There is no server-side account to delete.

Children's privacy

Paint Roll is a general-audience casual puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If Paint Roll is marked as designed for children in its Google Play "Target audience" declaration, ad serving will be restricted to comply with Google Play Families Policy and applicable laws (such as COPPA); please refer to the app's current Play Store listing for its target-audience declaration.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves (for example, when ads or analytics are actually enabled). Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
